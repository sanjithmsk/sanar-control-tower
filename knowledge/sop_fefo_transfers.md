# SOP-INV-02 FEFO and inter-branch transfers (sample)

Stock is dispensed and transferred First-Expiry-First-Out (FEFO): the batch with the earliest expiry date leaves first.

Batches with 30 days or less of shelf life remaining are excluded from replenishment planning and from transfers. They are dispensed locally first or returned.

A branch may donate only its surplus: FEFO stock plus in-transit stock, minus its own 14-day forecast and safety buffer. A transfer must never push the donating branch into deficit.

An internal transfer is preferred over a vendor purchase order whenever compliant surplus exists, because transit is measured in hours and vendor lead time in days.
