# Supplier agreements summary (sample)

Supplier A (primary): contracted lead time 3 days, list price, compliance score 98%. Default vendor for all medicines.

Supplier B (secondary): contracted lead time 5 days, price 8% above list, compliance score 94%. Used when Supplier A reports a shortage or misses its SLA.

Purchase orders are raised in full packs. Orders are rounded up to the pack size of the medicine.
