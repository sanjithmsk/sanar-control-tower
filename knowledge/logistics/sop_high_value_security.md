# SOP-SEC-01 High-value goods (sample)

Televisions and other high-value electronics are moved only in sealed, tracked vehicles with a secure-transport capability, and stored only in facilities with caged, camera-covered storage.

Electronics imports into Saudi Arabia need a valid SABER conformity certificate; the current certificate is SABER-PC-2026-55102.

Re-exports from the Jebel Ali free zone into Saudi Arabia are cross-border transfers: customs clearance into Saudi Arabia takes about 2 days and trade compliance approves each movement.
