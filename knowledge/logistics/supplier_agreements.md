# Supplier agreements summary (sample)

Gulf Electronics Distribution (UAE): primary electronics distributor. Lead time 4 days plus Saudi customs, 5% above list, freight 2%, compliance 95%. Ships DAP Riyadh or Dammam. Dangerous-goods certified and uses secure transport.

Shenzhen PowerTech (China): manufacturer. Lead time 21 days by sea plus customs, 30% below list, freight 8%, compliance 92%. Ships FOB Shenzhen; the buyer arranges the main carriage and insurance.

Saudi Fresh Foods: primary domestic food supplier. Lead time 2 days, 12% above list, compliance 93%. Frozen cold chain and halal certified. Delivers DDP.

Brazil Poultry Export SA: frozen poultry exporter. Lead time 18 days by reefer container plus customs, 28% below list, freight 9%, compliance 94%. Halal certified with a certified frozen cold chain. Ships CFR Jeddah.

Andalusia Olive Oils (Spain): lead time 12 days plus customs, 20% below list, freight 7%, compliance 96%. Ships CIF Jeddah.

Gulf Chem Trading (UAE): industrial chemicals. Lead time 5 days plus customs, list price, freight 3%, compliance 91%. Dangerous-goods certified.

Riyadh Apparel Supply: lead time 3 days, compliance score 88%, below the 90% minimum, so it is blocked until it passes re-audit. No other apparel supplier is qualified.
