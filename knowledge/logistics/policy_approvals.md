# POL-APP-01 Approval limits and roles (sample)

Any single action above SAR 50,000 needs the operations manager. Imports and cross-border transfers need the trade compliance officer or the operations manager, who confirms permits, HS codes and Incoterms.

Orders more than 15% above list price need the operations manager. Orders covering more than 90 days of demand need the operations manager or a supply planner.

Routine domestic actions within these limits may be executed automatically, up to the daily auto-execution budget of SAR 150,000.

Quarantine releases are signed by QA / food safety. Recalls are raised by QA / food safety or trade compliance.
