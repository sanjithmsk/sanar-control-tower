# POL-FD-02 Food imports and halal sourcing (sample)

Food may only be imported into Saudi Arabia against a valid SFDA food import registration for the product category. The current registration is SFDA-FIR-2026-7781. Food may not be moved into another country without that country's import registration.

Meat and poultry must come from halal-certified suppliers. A supplier without a halal certificate is never used for these products, whatever its price or lead time.

Customs duty on frozen poultry is 10% and on olive oil 5% of the customs value in this sample.
