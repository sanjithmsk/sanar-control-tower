# SOP-TC-01 Frozen food handling (sample)

Frozen food must stay at or below −18 °C in storage and in transit. Reefer trucks with data loggers are required on every lane that carries frozen goods.

Frozen transfers must arrive within 24 hours, including any customs dwell time at a border, because reefer units are not monitored while queued at customs.

On receipt the logger trace is checked. Any reading above −18 °C for more than 60 minutes quarantines the lot until QA / food safety has reviewed it.

Frozen food may only be bought from suppliers with a certified frozen cold chain and, for meat and poultry, a halal certificate.
