# SOP-DG-01 Dangerous goods (sample)

Lithium-ion power banks (UN3480) and flammable solvents (UN1993) are dangerous goods. They may only be stored at facilities licensed for dangerous goods and moved on lanes whose vehicles and drivers are ADR-certified; sea legs follow the IMDG code.

Dammam DC has no dangerous-goods licence, so dangerous goods are not stocked there or routed to it.

Suppliers of dangerous goods must hold dangerous-goods certification and provide a dangerous goods declaration with every shipment.
