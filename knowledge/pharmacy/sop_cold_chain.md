# SOP-CC-01 Cold-chain handling (sample)

Cold-chain medicines (insulins, biologics) must stay between 2 °C and 8 °C in storage and in transit.

Inter-branch transfers of cold-chain medicines may only use routes served by a validated refrigerated (reefer) vehicle with a data logger. If no reefer route exists, the transfer must not be raised and stock is sourced from a vendor instead.

Cold-chain transfers must arrive within 6 hours of dispatch. Longer journeys are sourced from a supplier with a validated cold chain instead.

On receipt the branch pharmacist checks the logger trace. Any excursion above 8 °C or below 2 °C for more than 30 minutes, or any reading below 0 °C (freezing), quarantines the batch until QA has reviewed it.

Suppliers of cold-chain medicines must hold cold-chain certification.
