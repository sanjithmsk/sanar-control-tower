# SOP-DG-01 Flammable products in branches (sample)

Alcohol-based hand sanitizer (70% alcohol) is a flammable liquid (UN1170, class 3) and is handled as dangerous goods.

It may only be stored at sites with an approved flammables cabinet and moved on lanes whose vehicles are cleared for dangerous goods. East Branch has no flammables storage and does not stock these products.

Suppliers of flammable products must be certified for dangerous-goods handling.
