# POL-PH-07 Therapeutic substitution during shortages (sample)

When a medicine cannot be supplied before the projected stockout, the control tower lists approved alternatives, such as a biosimilar or a generic equivalent, together with where they are in stock and whether they can be moved.

Substitution is never executed automatically. The pharmacist decides, notifies the prescriber and documents the interchange; biosimilar insulin interchange also needs the patient to be counselled.

Controlled substances are never substituted by the platform.
