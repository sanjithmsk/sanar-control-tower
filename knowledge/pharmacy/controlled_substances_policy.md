# POL-CS-03 Controlled substances and approval limits (sample)

Any transfer or purchase order for a controlled substance (for example tramadol) requires sign-off by a licensed pharmacist or the chief pharmacist before execution, with a batch-level custody record.

Controlled substances may only be stored at, and moved between, branches licensed for controlled substances, and only bought from suppliers licensed to supply them.

Any single action with a value above SAR 10,000 requires sign-off by the chief pharmacist or logistics manager. An action that is both controlled and above the limit therefore needs the chief pharmacist.

Routine actions below the limit that do not involve controlled substances may be executed automatically by the platform, up to the daily auto-execution budget, and are recorded in the activity log.

Batch releases from quarantine are signed by the QA officer or the chief pharmacist. Recalls are raised by the QA officer or the chief pharmacist.
