# POL-SFDA-01 Importing registered medicines and devices (sample)

Medicines and medical devices may only be imported into Saudi Arabia against a valid SFDA import permit for the product category. The permit must still be valid on the expected arrival date.

The device import permit SFDA-MDIP-2026-0147 covers blood glucose meters from MedTech GmbH. There is no medicine import permit on file, so medicines are sourced from domestic suppliers.

Customs clearance into Saudi Arabia takes 2 days on average and is added to the supplier lead time. Duty on medical devices is 5% of the customs value in this sample.
