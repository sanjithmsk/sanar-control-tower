# Supplier agreements summary (sample)

Supplier A (primary): contracted lead time 3 days, list price, compliance score 98%. Default vendor for medicines and consumer products. Cold-chain, controlled-substance and dangerous-goods certified.

Supplier B (secondary): contracted lead time 5 days, price 8% above list, compliance score 94%. Used when Supplier A reports a shortage or misses its SLA. Also distributes medical devices. Cold-chain, controlled-substance and dangerous-goods certified.

Supplier C (spot wholesaler): lead time 2 days, price 25% above list, compliance score 85%. Below the minimum compliance score, so it is not used unless the policy is changed. Holds no special-handling certifications.

MedTech GmbH (Germany): manufacturer of blood glucose meters. Lead time 7 days by air freight plus Saudi customs clearance, price 20% below list before 6% freight and 5% duty, compliance score 97%. Ships FCA Frankfurt; imports need the SFDA medical device import permit on file.

Purchase orders are raised in full packs. Orders are rounded up to the pack size of the item.
