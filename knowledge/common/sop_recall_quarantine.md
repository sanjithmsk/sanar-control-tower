# SOP-QA-04 Recalls and quarantine (sample)

A recall is raised by lot number. Every unit of the lot is quarantined across the network at once, including units on a transfer that is still in transit. Those units no longer count as incoming stock, so the receiving site is re-planned immediately.

Quarantined stock cannot be issued, planned or transferred. Stock is quarantined when it is recalled, when it passes its expiry date, or when a temperature excursion is found on receipt.

Only the quality assurance roles may release a quarantined lot back to stock, after investigation. Recalled and expired lots can never be released.

Held stock is disposed of by destruction or by return to the supplier. Every disposal is recorded with its value as a write-off in the activity log.
