# SOP-INV-02 FEFO, inter-site transfers and storage (sample)

Stock is issued and transferred First-Expiry-First-Out (FEFO): the lot with the earliest expiry date leaves first. Lots without an expiry date are issued after dated lots, oldest first.

Lots at or below the minimum remaining shelf life set in the guardrail policy (30 days for medicines, 14 days for food and general goods) are excluded from replenishment planning and from transfers. They are issued locally first or returned.

A site may donate only its surplus: FEFO stock plus in-transit stock, minus its own 14-day forecast and safety buffer. A transfer must never push the donating site into deficit.

An internal transfer is preferred over a purchase order whenever compliant surplus exists, because transit is measured in hours and supplier lead time in days. When a donor holds stock projected to expire unsold, that donor is used first if it can still arrive before the receiving site runs out.

A transfer is planned only for the quantity the receiving site can sell before the lot expires, without pushing any of its own lots past expiry. Sites with a tracked storage capacity cannot receive more than their free space.
