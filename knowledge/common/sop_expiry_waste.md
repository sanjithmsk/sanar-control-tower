# SOP-INV-05 Expiry risk, waste prevention and overstock (sample)

Expiry risk is projected for every lot by serving forecast demand earliest-expiry first. Units that would still be on the shelf on their expiry date are reported as projected waste, with their value.

Lots with projected waste and more than the minimum remaining shelf life are proposed for redistribution to a site that can sell them before expiry. The receiving site must not end up with projected waste of its own, and the donor keeps its forecast and safety buffer. Sites with a deficit are preferred, then the transfer that saves the most units, then the shortest transit.

Redistribution transfers follow the same handling, border and approval rules as any other transfer.

Lots inside the expiry watch window are listed daily so that they are issued first. Lots that pass their expiry date are moved to quarantine automatically and their value is recorded as a write-off.

A site with more days on hand than the overstock threshold is flagged, with the value tied up, so that replenishment is slowed and surplus is offered to other sites.
