# POL-CS-03 Controlled substances and approval limits (sample)

Any transfer or purchase order for a controlled substance (for example tramadol) requires sign-off by a licensed pharmacist before execution, with a batch-level custody record.

Any single action with a value above SAR 10,000 requires sign-off by the chief pharmacist or logistics manager.

Routine actions below the limit that do not involve controlled substances may be executed automatically by the platform and are recorded in the activity log.
